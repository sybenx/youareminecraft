What it was

You Are Minecraft ran on CraftBukkit 1.2.5 in 2012. Everyone who joined got a plot along the street and built a giant statue of their own skin, block by block.

Walking around

  • Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Zoom in on a street and every statue's name appears on its stand, just like the signs in game. Get closer still to read the signs players left around their builds.
  • Tap the magnifying glass to search all 86,896 names, or every sign in the world.
  • Use the menu to switch between perspective, flat and free-flight views.
  • The world is huge. Tiles load as you move, so give it a moment on a slow connection.

How this map was made

The original world was converted to a modern format with Chunker and rendered with BlueMap, using a resource pack that puts the 1.2.5 textures back on every block so it looks the way it did in 2012. The scripts and steps are in the GitHub repo.
